# 🎲 Ludo — Morgan Okoth

A classic fully-animated Ludo board game built with pure HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No frameworks, no dependencies — one single file that works anywhere including mobile.

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Gameplay
- ✅ Full 4-player Ludo with verified 51-cell clockwise outer ring path
- ✅ Roll a **6** to bring a token out of the yard — bonus re-roll granted
- ✅ Player **manually selects** which token to move (tokens glow when clickable)
- ✅ Auto-skip when no valid move exists
- ✅ Step-by-step token animation along the cross arms
- ✅ **Capture logic** — land on an enemy and send them back to yard
- ✅ Safe/starred cells protect tokens from capture
- ✅ **Win detection** — first player to bring all 4 tokens home wins

### 👥 Multiplayer & AI
- ✅ Choose **2 to 4 players** from the lobby
- ✅ Any player slot can be set to **AI** — AI fills in for missing players
- ✅ AI plays smartly — prefers captures, moves furthest token
- ✅ Custom player names in lobby

### 🎲 Flying Dice
- ✅ **Physics-based die** — launches from your tap/click position
- ✅ Tumbles across the screen, bounces off edges, then settles on result
- ✅ Tap anywhere on screen, the TAP zone, or the ROLL button
- ✅ Die colour changes to match the current player (Red/Green/Yellow/Blue)
- ✅ Tap ripple effect on mobile

### 🎨 Visuals
- ✅ Animated golden **MORGANENTP** letters orbiting the board
- ✅ Wooden frame with **MORGAN OKOTH** engraved on all four margins
- ✅ Realistic 15×15 board — correct quadrant colours, home circles, safe stars, directional arrows
- ✅ Starfield background with glowing gold UI
- ✅ Cinzel Decorative font for premium feel

## 🎮 How to Play

1. Choose number of players (2-4) and set any slot to AI if needed
2. Enter player names and click **START GAME**
3. **Tap the flying die** or press **ROLL** on your turn
4. Roll a **6** to bring a token onto the board
5. After rolling, **click a glowing token** to move it
6. Travel clockwise around the board then down your coloured home lane
7. Land on an enemy to **capture** them (starred cells are safe)
8. First player to get all **4 tokens to the centre** wins! 🏆
